At 6pm tonight (and on the second Wednesday of each month) we will again be screening a free spiritual movies at the Roseville New Church (4 Shirley Road, Roseville NSW).

Movies are selected based on a spiritual theme or by film-makers who were influenced by Emanuel Swedenborg's writings.

Tonight we screen the movie "Peacful Warrior," which can be read as a modern parable of regeneration: a gifted young gymnast is brought to crisis and, through a demanding mentor (Nick Nolte), is led to confront the illusions of self-reliance, pride, and outward success. As his ambitions are stripped back, he begins to discover a deeper order within - learning that true strength lies in aligning thought and action with a higher, inwardly perceived good. The film offers a clear depiction of temptation, reordering of the inner life, and the movement from external achievement to inward transformation, making it a strong basis for reflection on spiritual growth, the discipline of attention, and the life of use.

Join us to enjoy some free Popcorn, Jaffas, and Maltesers and drinks along with the movie.

Look forward to seeing you at these Roseville New Church movie nights on the second Wednesday of each month.

For a poster of the latest movie titles, [click here](https://static.swedenborg.com.au/pdf/fliers/SpiritualMovieNightsPoster.pdf).
