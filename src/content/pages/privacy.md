This page explains what this website collects about visitors and how we use it. The Swedenborg Centre is run by the Sydney Society of the New Church, 4 Shirley Road, Roseville NSW 2069.

# Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate, how the site is used: which pages are visited, roughly where in the world visitors are, what kind of device and browser they use, and how they arrived. Google Analytics sets cookies on your device to do this. We use the results to improve the site, and we do not use them to identify individual visitors.

We do not run advertising on this site and we do not sell information about visitors. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we tell Google not to use your visit for advertising. Your visit may still be counted in our aggregate figures.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Google explains how it handles this data in [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

# Hosting and files

The site is hosted by Vercel, and the audio and PDF files are served from a separate file host. Like most hosts, they keep standard server logs (such as your IP address and the pages or files requested) for security and performance.

# Events, enquiries and our mailing list

The events list is drawn from our public Google Calendar. If you email, phone or write to us, we use the details you give us to reply and to look after your enquiry. Zoom sessions are run through Zoom, which has its own privacy practices.

If you sign up to our email list, we ask for your name and email address and the lists you want. We first email you a link to confirm, and only then add you. We keep your details in Planning Center, the database used by the Sydney Society of the New Church, and send the confirmation email through Postmark. We use your details to send you the news you chose, and every email has an unsubscribe link. You can ask us to remove you at any time.

# Donations and other websites

The Donate button takes you to Donorbox, which handles your payment details. We do not see or store your card details. This site also links to other websites and services, such as Facebook and YouTube. They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

# Questions, access and changes

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing [info@swedenborg.com.au](mailto:info@swedenborg.com.au) or phoning [+61 2 9416 2812](tel:+61294162812). We may update this page from time to time.
